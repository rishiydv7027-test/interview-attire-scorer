# Data Folder

## raw/
Unlabeled images collected for each attribute, sorted into subfolders:
- beard/
- hair/
- shirt_tuck/
- shoes/
- pants/

## labeled/
Same structure as raw/, but images here are manually tagged
(e.g. shirt_tuck/tucked/, shirt_tuck/untucked/) after labeling.

Note: Team photos alone won't be enough variety — collect from
multiple people, lighting conditions, and backgrounds.

This is Step 4 in the roadmap and is the most time-consuming step —
start collecting early, in parallel with Steps 1-3.
